01 / Enterprise AI platform · Secure Hybrid AI Network Environment

shAIne: from launch
to measured value.

Problem
Astrion had no enterprise AI capability and needed one that met defense security requirements.
Solution
Launched shAIne on Astrion’s internal Azure GCC High environment with governance in place from the first month: reuse controls, output quality and explainability standards. Built a value model that multiplies attributed hours by loaded labor rate, by role, so AI return is auditable.
Outcome
Full seat uptake within one quarter at about 77% weekly use. Savings from the first three instrumented users alone exceeded the platform’s full annual cost by 40%.

Problem
Leadership needed one current read on AI, Department of War, Counter-UAS, and contract news spread across dozens of sources.
Solution
Built an agent that monitors 39 federal, defense, and AI sources through the day, scores each item for business relevance, and explains why. It tracks 15 themes, including Department of War contracts, Counter-UAS, AI policy, and a recompete radar.
