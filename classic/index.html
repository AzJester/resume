Enterprise AI platform · Astrion

shAIne: from launch to measured value.

Problem. Astrion had no enterprise AI capability and needed one that met defense security requirements.

Solution. Launched shAIne (Secure Hybrid AI Network Environment) on Astrion’s internal Azure GCC High environment with governance in place from the first month: reuse controls, output quality and explainability standards. Built a value model that multiplies attributed hours by loaded labor rate, by role, so AI return is auditable.

Outcome. Full seat uptake within one quarter at about 77% weekly use. Savings from the first three instrumented users alone exceeded the platform’s full annual cost by 40%.

In the platform’s first week, agentic AI coding delivered a program’s status reporting tool and avoided about 310 hours of development.

Agentic AI · Defense, AI & contract intelligence

AI Intel Brief Agent

Problem. Leadership needed one current read on AI, Department of War, Counter-UAS, and contract news spread across dozens of sources.

Solution. Built an agent that monitors 39 federal, defense, and AI sources through the day, scores each item for business relevance, and explains why. It tracks 15 themes, including Department of War contracts, Counter-UAS, AI policy, and a recompete radar.

Outcome. One relevance-ranked feed with a daily snapshot, weekly brief, and searchable archive.

Agentic AI · Executive decision support

Enterprise Intelligence Command Center

Problem. Executives waited days, and sometimes weeks, on manual reports for financial and pipeline answers.

Solution. Built an AI Assistant that lets the CEO and executive leaders direct agents to generate reports and forecast financials and pipeline needs, with alerts on team-defined issues and risks in integration. It runs on a hybrid on-premises and Azure data foundation: Data Factory, medallion data lake, Synapse, row-level security, and Entra ID.

Outcome. Goes live December 4, 2026, replacing days- and weeks-long manual reporting with on-demand answers.
