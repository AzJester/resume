to a usable value model.

Challenge
Put enterprise AI to work and make its business value explainable.
My responsibility
Platform launch, AI strategy, ModelOps, evidence governance, and value measurement.
Result
Stood up the platform with governance from the first month: reuse controls, output quality and explainability standards, and a role-based method for attributing the hours it saves.
A working reporting tool, delivered in the first week.

Applied agentic AI coding to build a Weekly Status Reporting tool. The project estimate attributed 310 hours of avoided development effort, valued at approximately $32,500 in loaded labor cost.

This separate project estimate is not added to the platform figures.

Method and limits

The $53K–$73K range comes from the initial deployment value model. Individual role rates and the underlying activity ledger are not published here.

The annual subscription is a separate cost reference. A net ROI calculation would need the same measurement period for benefits and costs, including implementation and ongoing support.

The reporting-tool figure is a separate development-effort estimate. No combined savings total is claimed.
